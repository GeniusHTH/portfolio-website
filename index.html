What I'm working on

UrgeGuard

A mobile app for people recovering from addiction. It's a panic button that doesn't wait for you to be in the right state of mind to press it: it reads signals from a wearable to notice when an urge might be building, walks you through a breathing exercise, and can alert someone you trust.

I went through an addiction phase myself, and I know how hard it is to stop. I built UrgeGuard to help me stay on track, and to make that kind of help easy to reach for anyone else who needs it.
